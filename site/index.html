Common questions

Quick answers about who we work with, how pricing works and what to share first.

Enquire
Who do you work with?

Homeowners, interior designers, contractors, modular furniture businesses, students and professionals who need focused technical support.

Can you support projects remotely?

Yes. Online consultancy covers projects across India. On-site services are available in Hyderabad and Bengaluru, depending on location and availability.

What should I share first?

Your city, project type, approximate area, current stage, the main challenge, the service you want and your target timeline. Each service page lists what helps most.

How is a service priced?

Site measurement is ₹4 per sq. ft. (minimum booking ₹3,000). Homeowner consulting is 10% of total project cost online or 15% on-site. Other services are quoted by scope. Taxes are extra where applicable.

Can you work alongside my own contractor?

Yes. Homeowner consulting is designed for projects built by your own vendors or contractors. You get an independent opinion on quotations, drawings, quality and handover.
