Who do you work with?

We work with home owners seeking independent quality checks, interior designers needing accurate site drawings, modular contractors requiring factory cutting lists, and students learning modular woodwork.

Can you support projects remotely?

Yes. Remote consulting (drawing reviews, quotation audits, and BOQ analysis) is available pan-India. On-site laser site measurements and physical inspections are conducted across Hyderabad and Bengaluru.

What should I share first?

Simply share your property city, layout type (apartment, villa, or office), approximate carpet area, current project stage, and the decision you need guidance on. We will recommend the exact technical scope for your requirements.

How is a service priced?

Site measurement is ₹4 per sq. ft. of built-up area (minimum booking ₹3,000). End-to-end home owner consulting is 10% of total project cost (online pan-India) or 15% (on-site in Hyderabad & Bengaluru). Other services are quoted transparently based on scope with zero hidden markups.

Can you work alongside my existing contractor?

Yes — that is where we add the greatest value. We do not sell furniture or accept contractor commissions. We work solely on your behalf to audit contractor estimates, inspect delivered material grades, and verify workmanship before you release payments.
